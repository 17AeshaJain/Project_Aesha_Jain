Nutrition Basics

Fuel Your Progress

You can't out-train a bad diet. Here are the fundamentals every beginner needs to know.

🥩

Protein First

Aim for 0.7–1g of protein per pound of body weight. Chicken, eggs, lentils, paneer, and tofu are great sources.

🍚

Smart Carbs

Whole grains, oats, sweet potatoes, and rice give sustained energy for workouts. Eat them around training.

💧

Hydration

Drink 2.5–3 litres of water daily. Add 500ml per hour of exercise. Dehydration kills performance instantly.

🥑

Healthy Fats

Avocados, nuts, seeds, and olive oil support hormone balance, brain health, and joint function.

🍎

Fruits & Veggies

Fill half your plate with vegetables. They provide micronutrients, fibre, and antioxidants that aid recovery.

⏰

Meal Timing

Eat a light meal 1–2 hours before training. Have protein + carbs within 45 minutes post-workout for recovery.

🚫

Limit Junk

80/20 rule: eat clean 80% of the time. One indulgent meal won't ruin progress — but daily junk food will.

😴

Sleep & Recovery

Muscles grow during sleep, not during workouts. Aim for 7–9 hours. Poor sleep undoes hard gym work.
