FAQ

Common Questions

Quick answers before you hit send.

Do I need equipment?

Not at all. Our Home Workout track is 100% bodyweight. The gym track is separate and clearly labelled.

Is FitStart really free?

Yes — completely. No sign-up, no subscription, no ads. It's a student project built to genuinely help beginners.

I'm a complete beginner — where do I start?

Head to the Home Workout page and start with Level 1. Do it 3× per week for 4 weeks before moving up.

How often should I work out?

3–4 days per week is ideal for beginners. Rest and recovery are just as important as the workout itself.

Can I do yoga and workouts on the same day?

Absolutely. Yoga is great as a warm-up or cool-down. A short yoga session after strength training aids recovery.

Will my data be stored?

No. This form is for demonstration purposes as part of a web design project. No data is stored or transmitted.
