🏠 HOME WORKOUTS

Zero equipment. Maximum results. These bodyweight routines are built for small spaces, busy schedules, and absolute beginners. Your bedroom is now your gym.

Start Here Every Time

Warm-Up Routine (5 min)

Never skip this. A proper warm-up primes your joints, raises your heart rate, and dramatically cuts injury risk.

01

March in Place

Lift knees to hip height · Swing arms naturally · 60 seconds. Gets your heart rate going gently.

1 min
02

Arm Circles

10 forward, 10 backward · Gradually increase circle size. Warms up shoulders and upper back.

20 reps
03

Hip Circles

Stand feet shoulder-width, hands on hips · Draw big circles with your hips · 10 each direction.

20 reps
04

Ankle & Wrist Rolls

10 circles each joint in each direction. Essential before floor exercises and push-up variations.

40 reps
05

Leg Swings

Hold a wall for balance · Swing each leg forward/back 10 times, then side-to-side 10 times. Opens up hips.

20 reps/leg

Level 1

Beginner Full Body (Week 1–4)

Do 3 rounds. Rest 60 seconds between each exercise. Rest 90 seconds between rounds. Focus on form.

01

Bodyweight Squat

Feet shoulder-width apart · Toes slightly out · Push hips back and down · Keep chest tall · Drive through heels to stand. The king of leg exercises.

3×10
02

Knee Push-Up

Hands slightly wider than shoulders · Lower chest to floor slowly · Push back up · Keep core tight. Great entry to upper body pushing.

3×8
03

Glute Bridge

Lie on back · Knees bent, feet flat · Drive hips toward ceiling · Squeeze glutes at top · Hold 1 sec · Lower slowly. Builds glutes and lower back.

3×12
04

Dead Bug

Lie on back · Arms up toward ceiling · Lower opposite arm and leg toward floor · Return to start · Switch sides. Outstanding core stability exercise.

3×6/side
05

Superman Hold

Lie face down · Arms extended · Lift arms, chest and legs off floor simultaneously · Hold 2 seconds · Lower. Builds entire posterior chain.

3×8
06

Plank Hold

Forearms on floor · Body straight from head to heels · Engage core, glutes, and quads · Breathe normally. The ultimate anti-extension core exercise.

3×20 sec
07

Jumping Jacks

Jump feet wide while raising arms overhead · Return to start · Keep a steady, controlled pace · Great cardio finisher for this level.

3×20

Level 2

Intermediate Home Workout (Week 5–8)

Step it up. 4 rounds. Rest 45–60 seconds between exercises. These are progressions of what you mastered in Level 1.

01

Full Push-Up

Graduate from knee push-ups · Straight body, toes on floor · Chest touches or nearly touches floor · Explosive push up. Build your chest and triceps seriously.

4×10
02

Jump Squat

Start in squat position · Explode upward · Land softly with bent knees · Immediately go into next rep. Builds power and burns calories fast.

4×10
03

Reverse Lunge

Step one foot back · Lower back knee toward floor · Front knee tracks over toes · Push front foot to return. More knee-friendly than forward lunges.

4×10/leg
04

Pike Push-Up

Form an inverted V with your body · Bend elbows and lower head toward floor · Push up. Targets shoulders heavily — beginner overhead press alternative.

4×8
05

Mountain Climbers

High plank position · Drive one knee toward chest rapidly · Switch legs quickly · Keep hips level. Full-body cardio + core combo.

4×30 sec
06

Single-Leg Glute Bridge

Lift one leg off floor · Drive single-leg hip bridge · Squeeze glute at top · Hold 1 second. Much harder than the regular version — isolates each glute.

4×10/leg
07

Tricep Dips (Chair)

Hands on edge of chair behind you · Lower body by bending elbows · Push back up · Keep elbows pointed backward, not flared. Isolates triceps nicely.

4×12
08

Burpee

Stand → Squat → Jump back to push-up → Push up → Jump feet forward → Jump up with hands overhead. The most efficient full-body exercise in existence.

4×8

Level 3

Advanced Home Workout (Week 9+)

You've earned it. 4–5 rounds. 30–45 sec rest between exercises. These movements will genuinely challenge you.

01

Diamond Push-Up

Hands form a diamond under chest · Elbows flare back · Lower chest to hands · Press back up. Maximum tricep and inner chest activation.

5×10
02

Pistol Squat (Assisted)

Hold a doorframe or wall · Extend one leg forward · Squat down on one leg · Drive up through heel. One of the hardest single-leg movements you can do.

4×6/leg
03

Archer Push-Up

Wide hand position · Shift weight to one arm while the other extends · Lower chest to the bent arm · Alternate sides. Great step toward one-arm push-up.

4×6/side
04

Plyometric Lunge

Start in lunge · Jump and switch legs in the air · Land softly in lunge on opposite side · Immediately repeat. Extreme leg power builder.

4×10/leg
05

Hollow Body Hold

Lie on back · Press lower back into floor · Extend arms overhead and legs out · Hold position while breathing. Elite gymnast core exercise — incredibly difficult.

4×30 sec
06

Pseudo Planche Push-Up

Place hands at hip level with fingers pointing back · Lean forward heavily · Do push-up while angled. Builds the shoulder and chest strength needed for calisthenics skills.

4×8

Cardio Blast

20-Minute Home HIIT

Work : Rest = 40 seconds on · 20 seconds off. 4 rounds of these 5 exercises. No equipment. All out.

🔥
High Knees

Drive knees up to hip height as fast as possible. Pump your arms. This is your sprint replacement indoors.

⚡
Jump Squats

Squat down, explode up, land soft. The landing is just as important as the jump — control it every single rep.

💥
Push-Up to T-Rotation

Do a push-up, then rotate into a side plank with arm extended. Alternating sides. Chest + core + shoulder stabiliser.

🌀
Skater Hops

Leap sideways from one foot to the other, landing softly. Mimics speed skating. Great for coordination and hip strength.

🏃
Burpee

The finisher of all finishers. Every rep engages your entire body. Pace yourself but keep moving — don't stop.

Finish Every Session

Cool-Down Stretches (5 min)

01

Standing Quad Stretch

Stand on one leg · Pull opposite foot to glute · Hold · Switch. Hold 30 seconds each side. Essential after all the squats and lunges.

30s/side
02

Seated Hamstring Stretch

Sit with legs extended · Reach forward toward toes · Hold · Don't bounce. Relieves the lower back and hamstrings worked during squats.

30s
03

Child's Pose

Kneel and sit back on heels · Extend arms forward · Rest forehead on floor · Breathe deeply. The best full-back release you can do.

45s
04

Chest Opener

Clasp hands behind back · Squeeze shoulder blades · Lift hands slightly · Open chest · Hold. Counters the rounded position of push-ups.

30s
05

Deep Diaphragmatic Breathing

Lie flat · Hands on belly · Inhale 4 counts (belly rises) · Hold 4 · Exhale 6 · Repeat 5 times. Lowers cortisol and signals recovery mode.

5 breaths
