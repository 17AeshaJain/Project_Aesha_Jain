🧘 YOGA & FLEXIBILITY

Calm the mind. Open the body. Yoga isn't just stretching — it's a complete system for strength, flexibility, breathing, and mental clarity. Perfect for beginners.

Benefits

Why Every Fitness Plan Needs Yoga

Even 15 minutes of yoga daily produces measurable improvements in flexibility, posture, and stress levels within 4 weeks.

🌿
Improved Flexibility

Regular yoga lengthens muscles and connective tissue, dramatically reducing injury risk in your other workouts and daily life.

🧠
Stress Reduction

Controlled breathing activates the parasympathetic nervous system, lowering cortisol and helping you manage academic and life stress.

🏛️
Better Posture

Most students have tech neck and hunched shoulders from hours of screen time. Yoga directly corrects these postural imbalances.

💤
Better Sleep

An evening yoga practice lowers heart rate and body temperature — two key triggers for quality sleep. Crucial for exam periods.

💪
Core Strength

Many yoga poses require sustained core activation. Yoga practitioners often have exceptional core stability without doing a single crunch.

🫁
Breath Control

Pranayama (breathing techniques) improve lung capacity, focus, and mental clarity — skills that transfer to every area of life.

Foundation Flow

Surya Namaskar (Sun Salutation)

The classic 12-pose sequence that warms up every muscle in the body. Do 3–5 rounds as your morning wake-up or workout warm-up. Takes about 5 minutes.

01

Pranamasana — Prayer Pose

Stand upright · Bring palms together at chest · Close eyes · Take 3 deep breaths. Centres your mind and sets your intention for the practice.

3 breaths
02

Hasta Uttanasana — Raised Arms

Inhale · Raise arms overhead · Arch back gently · Look up · Stretch through entire front body. Opens chest and stretches the abdomen.

Inhale
03

Hasta Padasana — Standing Forward Fold

Exhale · Fold forward from hips · Bring hands to floor beside feet · Let head hang heavy. Stretches entire back chain — hamstrings, back, neck.

Exhale
04

Ashwa Sanchalanasana — Equestrian Pose

Inhale · Step right foot back into lunge · Left knee bent 90° · Look up. Deep hip flexor stretch — great for those who sit all day.

Inhale
05

Dandasana — Plank

Exhale · Step left foot back · Body forms straight line · Core tight · Wrists under shoulders. Builds core and arm strength while completing the transition.

Hold
06

Ashtanga Namaskara — Eight-Limb Pose

Lower knees, chest, and chin to floor simultaneously · Hips stay up · Elbows stay close. A graceful beginner alternative to a full push-up lowering.

Exhale
07

Bhujangasana — Cobra Pose

Inhale · Press hands into floor · Lift chest and head · Elbows slightly bent · Look forward or up. Opens the chest, strengthens the spine, and stretches the abdomen.

Inhale
08

Adho Mukha Svanasana — Downward Dog

Exhale · Push hips up and back · Straighten legs · Press heels toward floor · Form an inverted V. One of yoga's most complete poses — stretches and strengthens simultaneously.

5 breaths

Complete the sequence by mirroring steps 4→1 on the left side. That's one full round.

Core Poses

15 Essential Beginner Poses

Master these and you'll have the foundation to follow any beginner yoga class or routine.

🧘

Mountain Pose

Tadasana

Stand tall with feet hip-width · Arms at sides · Ground through all four corners of each foot · Shoulders back and down. The foundation of all standing poses — deceptively powerful.

Standing Beginner
🌳

Tree Pose

Vrikshasana

Stand on one foot · Place other foot on inner thigh or calf (not knee) · Bring palms to chest · Balance · Breathe. Builds single-leg balance and focus simultaneously.

Balance Beginner
🐱

Cat-Cow

Marjaryasana-Bitilasana

On all fours · Inhale: arch back, lift head (Cow) · Exhale: round spine, tuck chin (Cat) · Repeat slowly. Incredible for spinal mobility and lower back pain relief.

Spine Beginner
🐍

Cobra Pose

Bhujangasana

Lie face down · Place palms by shoulders · Inhale and lift chest · Elbows soft · Look forward. Counteracts all the forward rounding of sitting and studying.

Backbend Beginner
🌉

Bridge Pose

Setu Bandhasana

Lie on back · Knees bent · Feet flat · Press into floor and lift hips · Squeeze glutes · Clasp hands under back. Strengthens glutes and opens hip flexors — perfect post-run or post-class.

Hip Opener Beginner
🦋

Butterfly Pose

Baddha Konasana

Sit on floor · Bring soles of feet together · Hold feet · Gently press knees toward floor · Fold forward slightly. Deep inner thigh and hip opener — great for desk-bound people.

Hip Opener Beginner
🐕

Downward Dog

Adho Mukha Svanasana

Form inverted V · Hips high · Heels press toward floor · Head between arms · Spread fingers wide. Stretches hamstrings and calves while building arm and core strength.

Full Body Moderate
👶

Child's Pose

Balasana

Kneel and sit back on heels · Extend arms forward · Rest forehead on mat · Breathe into the back. The universal "reset" pose — use it any time you need to rest during practice.

Rest Beginner
⚔️

Warrior I

Virabhadrasana I

Step foot forward into deep lunge · Back foot at 45° · Raise arms overhead · Square hips forward · Gaze up. Builds strength in legs and opens chest powerfully.

Standing Moderate
🦅

Warrior II

Virabhadrasana II

Wide stance · Front knee bent over ankle · Arms extended parallel to floor · Gaze over front hand. Builds hip and thigh strength — one of yoga's most powerful standing poses.

Standing Moderate
🌊

Pigeon Pose

Eka Pada Rajakapotasana

From downward dog · Bring one knee forward behind wrist · Extend other leg back · Lower torso forward. The deepest hip opener in beginner yoga — especially good after leg day at gym.

Hip Opener Moderate
🌀

Seated Spinal Twist

Ardha Matsyendrasana

Sit with one leg extended · Cross other foot over · Twist toward raised knee · Use arm as a lever · Breathe deeply. Detoxifies organs and relieves back tension.

Twist Beginner
🙏

Legs Up the Wall

Viparita Karani

Sit close to wall · Swing legs up · Let back rest on floor · Arms out · Close eyes · Stay 5 minutes. Reverses blood flow, reduces swelling in legs, and deeply calms the nervous system.

Restorative Beginner
⭐

Star Pose

Utthita Tadasana

Stand with feet wide · Extend arms out to sides · Spread fingers · Feel wide and expansive · Take 5 deep breaths. A confidence-boosting opener before any practice or exam.

Standing Beginner
💤

Corpse Pose

Savasana

Lie flat on back · Arms slightly away from body · Palms up · Eyes closed · Completely release all tension · Stay 5–10 minutes. The most important pose in yoga — never skip it.

Relaxation Beginner

Pranayama

Breathing Techniques

Breath is the foundation of yoga. These techniques can be practiced anywhere — in class, before exams, or while falling asleep.

01

Box Breathing (4-4-4-4)

Inhale 4 counts · Hold 4 counts · Exhale 4 counts · Hold 4 counts. Used by elite athletes and military personnel to control stress response. Do 4–6 rounds before any challenging situation.

4 min
02

4-7-8 Breathing

Inhale 4 counts · Hold 7 counts · Exhale 8 counts. Clinically shown to reduce anxiety and aid sleep onset. Do before bed for dramatically improved sleep quality.

3 min
03

Nadi Shodhana (Alternate Nostril)

Close right nostril · Inhale left · Close left · Exhale right · Inhale right · Close right · Exhale left. One round. Balances the brain hemispheres — improves focus and calm simultaneously.

5 min
04

Kapalabhati (Skull Shining)

Short, sharp exhales through the nose · Passive inhales between each · 30 pumps, then 1 long exhale. Energising — like a shot of espresso without caffeine. Do in the morning.

2 min

Daily Routines

Morning & Evening Flows

Two 15-minute sequences designed around different times of day and energy levels.

🌅

Morning Flow (15 min)

Energise and activate. Do this before breakfast to wake up your body and set a positive tone.

1. Cat-Cow × 10 breaths
2. Downward Dog · Hold 5 breaths
3. Sun Salutation A × 3 rounds
4. Warrior I + II each side
5. Tree Pose · 5 breaths each side
6. Mountain Pose + Kapalabhati breathing
🌙

Evening Flow (15 min)

Wind down and release. Do this 1 hour before bed to calm your nervous system and improve sleep quality.

1. Child's Pose · 10 deep breaths
2. Butterfly Pose · 2 minutes
3. Pigeon Pose · 2 min each side
4. Seated Spinal Twist each side
5. Legs Up the Wall · 5 minutes
6. Savasana + 4-7-8 breathing · 5 min

Mind & Body

Mental Health Benefits of Yoga

Science-backed reasons every student should practice yoga, especially during exam season.

🧠 Reduces Anxiety

Studies show 8 weeks of regular yoga reduces anxiety symptoms by up to 40%. Slow breathing activates the vagus nerve, your body's built-in calm switch.

📚 Improves Concentration

The focused attention required in yoga practice trains the prefrontal cortex — directly improving your ability to concentrate during lectures and exams.

😊 Boosts Mood

Physical movement + breathing exercises increase GABA, serotonin, and dopamine simultaneously — the triple-hit of feel-good neurotransmitters.

🌙 Fixes Sleep

Evening yoga reduces cortisol and core body temperature — both critical triggers for falling asleep faster and reaching deeper sleep stages.
